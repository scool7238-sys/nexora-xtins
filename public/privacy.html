Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

NEXORA XTINS is designed around practical local control. This policy explains what browser-related information the application can store, where it is stored, and how external websites and services fit into the picture.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. About NEXORA XTINS

NEXORA XTINS is a Windows desktop web browser developed and published by XTINS.

XTINS does not operate its own cloud service for storing your browser history, bookmarks, cookies, sessions, downloads, or other browser profile data. Browser-related data is designed to remain on your Windows device unless a particular feature explicitly communicates with an external service.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, NEXORA XTINS may store browser-related information locally on your Windows device, including:

  • Browsing history and recently visited pages
  • Bookmarks and saved browser settings
  • Download information
  • Cookies and website data managed by the WebView2 browser runtime
  • Session and tab information
  • Privacy and browser configuration settings

These items are part of the browser experience and are not automatically uploaded to XTINS servers.

3. Local protected storage

NEXORA XTINS includes a NEXORA-owned local storage layer for selected persistent browser data. The application describes this layer as protected with AES-256-GCM and Windows DPAPI.

Website data managed by the Microsoft WebView2 runtime, such as third-party cookies, site cache, and web page storage, remains within the WebView2 boundary and is distinct from NEXORA-owned application storage.

4. Third-party websites and services

When you browse the web with NEXORA XTINS, websites and online services you visit may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. XTINS does not control the privacy practices of those third parties.

Search engines, websites, downloads, embedded content, and other external services may therefore have their own data collection, cookies, analytics, or account systems.

5. Downloads and security

Files downloaded through NEXORA XTINS are stored on your Windows device according to your browser and Windows settings. XTINS does not guarantee that every file available from a third-party website is safe.

Keep Windows Security enabled. NEXORA XTINS is not a replacement for Windows Security, Microsoft Defender, or other appropriate security software. Keep your security software enabled and up to date, and use trusted sources when downloading files.

6. Personal information and selling data

XTINS does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Because NEXORA XTINS is designed with local browser storage, XTINS does not need to receive ordinary browser-profile data on an XTINS-operated cloud server for the browser to function.

7. Privacy controls

NEXORA XTINS provides browser privacy controls intended to help you manage browsing data and privacy-related behavior. You can use the controls available in the application to manage or clear supported local browser data.

8. Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as NEXORA XTINS changes or adds features. The latest version will be published on the official NEXORA XTINS website.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the official contact method published on the NEXORA XTINS website.
